Can I ask first?

Yes. You can send your space photos and basic concern first. REFIT will advise the suitable next step before you commit further.

02

What should I prepare?

Photos or videos, location, rough size, your main concern, budget direction and expected timing are enough to begin.

No. It is only for people who want REFIT to spend proper time reviewing their renovation concern before moving further.
